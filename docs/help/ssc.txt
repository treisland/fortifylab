SOFTWARE SECURITY CENTER (SSC)

SSC is the central Fortify application for organizing applications and
versions, receiving scan results, reviewing findings, managing users, and
integrating ScanCentral services. It persists data in MySQL.

Open SSC at https://ssc.$DOMAIN (substitute the configured domain). Initial
credentials are reported by SSC startup logs. ScanCentral SAST requires a
ControllerToken created in SSC; treat it as a secret and never paste it into
logs or support bundles.

SSC pod readiness alone does not prove login, database migration, or an
application endpoint is healthy. Use the wizard's status and troubleshooting
facilities when available.
