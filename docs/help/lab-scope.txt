LAB DEPLOYMENT VS FORTIFY PRODUCTS

The architecture and automation in this repository are intended only for
evaluation, demonstration, training, and isolated lab use. They are not a
production deployment design and do not provide production-grade availability,
hardening, monitoring, backup, disaster recovery, or support guarantees.

Do not store production source, customer data, real application credentials,
regulated data, or business-critical scan results here. Do not expose the lab
directly to untrusted networks. Scan only explicitly authorized targets.

This boundary applies to this repository's single-node lab architecture—not to
the production capabilities of Fortify products. Consult official Fortify
documentation and your organization's requirements for production design.
