SCANCENTRAL DAST

ScanCentral DAST assesses a running web application. DAST Core coordinates
scans and stores operational data in PostgreSQL; scanners send requests to the
authorized target. Scanners obtain licensing through LIM and communicate with
DAST Core. SSC integration supports broader application-security workflows.

Before scanning, configure a valid DAST license and the expected scanner pool
in LIM. Scan only systems you own or have explicit permission to test. Prefer a
deliberately vulnerable, isolated training target. DAST traffic can alter data,
trigger workflows, or disrupt a target even when the lab itself is isolated.
