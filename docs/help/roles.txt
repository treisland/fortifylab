ROLES AND LEARNING PATHS

Developer: learn applications/versions in SSC, submit approved training code
to SAST, then find and understand the resulting issues.

Security analyst: review and prioritize SSC findings, compare SAST and DAST
coverage, and practice reporting with synthetic lab data.

Lab operator: follow Guided deployment, understand dependency order, monitor
with view-only Dashboard access, and practice safe stop/retry/repair behavior.

DAST learner: configure LIM and a scanner pool, select an isolated authorized
target, and observe how DAST Core, scanners, PostgreSQL, LIM, and SSC interact.

All roles should first read Lab deployment vs Fortify products and URLs.
