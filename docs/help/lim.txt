LICENSE AND INFRASTRUCTURE MANAGER (LIM)

LIM manages Fortify dynamic-testing license capacity and scanner pools. In
this lab, DAST scanners contact LIM to acquire a seat from the configured pool.
LIM does not replace SSC and is not the interface for reviewing findings.

Open https://lim.$DOMAIN, upload the appropriate DAST license, and create the
pool named by LIM_POOL_NAME (Default in the sample configuration). License
files and LIM credentials are sensitive; help pages never read or display
them. After changing LIM configuration, retry or redeploy the DAST scanner.
